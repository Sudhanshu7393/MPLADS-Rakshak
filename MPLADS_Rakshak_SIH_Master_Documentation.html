🏛️ Executive Overview & Problem Statement

1. The ₹4,000 Crore Governance Challenge in MPLADS SLIDE 01

The Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS) allocates ₹5 Crore annually per MP (₹4,000+ Crore national annual outlay across 790+ MPs) for essential community infrastructure (rural roads, drinking water, healthcare).

🔴 1. Cost Inflation & Estimates

Identical works (e.g. 500m CC Road) sanctioned at +50% to +80% above neighboring district medians without automated peer benchmarking.

🟠 2. Duplicate & Ghost Projects

Multiple MPs or consecutive terms fund the same physical asset because titles differ slightly in text (e.g. "Shiva Temple Road" vs "Shiv Mandir Way").

🟣 3. Stalled Timelines & Delays

Works lingering 180–400+ days past sanctioned deadlines with funds disbursed without verifiable completion proof.

🟤 4. Contractor Cartels & Fake Photos

Single agencies capturing 70%+ district works; inspection photos uploaded from office desks (fake GPS & gallery uploads).

The Fundamental Flaw: Existing e-SAKSHI is a PASSIVE data store. Audits happen 2 years later after funds are spent. We need Real-Time Anomaly Interception.

💡 Proposed Solution: MPLADS Rakshak

2. Intelligent Decision-Support Shield for DPOs SLIDE 02

MPLADS Rakshak is an AI-powered, non-invasive decision support layer that intercepts e-SAKSHI proposal streams, calculates 0–100 risk scores across 6 calibrated anomaly engines, and delivers prioritized, court-ready dossiers to District Planning Officers.

⚡ Non-Invasive Feasibility

Plugs directly into e-SAKSHI APIs with zero database modifications or disruption to active workflows.

🎯 100% Explainable AI (XAI)

No black-box scores. Every flag gives exact numerical proof (e.g. +53.8% IQR cost, 195d lag, 92% NLP match).

📑 1-Click Court Dossiers

Generates MoSPI-compliant statutory PDF reports with digital audit trails for instant vigilance action.

🛡️ Judges Q&A Defense Guide (Top 5 Questions & Winning Answers)

6. Bulletproof Answers to Tough Questions Q&A DEFENSE
Q1: "Are you replacing e-SAKSHI or how does this integrate?"
Winning Answer: "No, Sir/Ma'am. e-SAKSHI is the official transactional ERP of the Government of India. MPLADS Rakshak is an auxiliary decision-support and intelligence layer that sits on top of e-SAKSHI APIs. It does not replace the database or approval workflow; it simply empowers District Officers with pre-triaged risk scores and mathematical evidence."
Q2: "What if the AI model gives a False Positive prediction?"
Winning Answer: "That is why we built 100% Explainable AI (XAI) with a strict Human-in-the-Loop architecture. The AI never automatically cancels or approves a project; it provides decomposed proof (IQR variance, delay days, distance mismatch) in the 360° Risk Passport. The final determination always rests with the District Planning Officer."
Q3: "How do you catch duplicate works if project titles use different words?"
Winning Answer: "We use a dual-tier matching engine: 1) TF-IDF N-gram NLP Vectorization to match semantic intent (e.g. 'CC Road near Temple' vs 'Paved Pathway at Mandir'), and 2) Haversine Geodesic Distance to confirm both works are within a <500m radius. This achieves >90% accuracy in duplicate detection."
Q4: "How does your Geo-Camera prevent officers from uploading gallery photos from home?"
Winning Answer: "File gallery uploads are blocked at the browser/mobile API level; only live camera streams are permitted. When the shutter fires, live hardware GPS is captured, distance from site is verified, and a SHA-256 cryptographic digest is immediately computed and locked into the immutable audit ledger."
Q5: "How does it scale to national level across 1,00,000 works?"
Winning Answer: "We implemented in-memory maps and Hibernate JDBC bulk batching (Batch size 100) in Spring Boot. 3,013 works are fully analyzed in 220 milliseconds. At national scale with 1,00,000 works, the batch processing completes in under 4 seconds on standard cloud infrastructure."
