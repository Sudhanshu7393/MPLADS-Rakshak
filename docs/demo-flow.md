## Step-by-Step Demo Walkthrough

### 1. The Scale & Problem Framing (0:00 - 0:30)
- **What to say**: *"Under MPLADS, over ₹4,000 Crore is authorized annually across thousands of projects. The government's eSAKSHI portal records every recommendation, sanction, and payment. But officers face information overload: with thousands of records, which work should they inspect first?"*
- **Action**: Show **Command Center** with 1,600 works, risk distribution donut, and top anomaly density districts.

---

### 2. The Prioritized Risk Queue (0:30 - 1:00)
- **What to say**: *"Instead of manual inspection, MPLADS Rakshak runs a multi-tier anomaly scan—combining peer-group cost benchmarks, milestone delays, ML Isolation Forest, and semantic similarity."*
- **Action**: Click **Risk Queue**; filter by `🔴 High Risk`. Point out how works are ranked from highest composite score down to lowest.

---

### 3. Deep-Dive into the Risk Passport (1:00 - 1:45)
- **What to say**: *"Let's inspect Work `MPL-2024-UP-004821` (CC Road in Rampur, Varanasi). Its risk score is 87/100. The score is not a black-box—here is the exact breakdown."*
- **Action**:
  1. Show **"Why this work was flagged"** (Cost outlier + 420m duplicate match + missing certificate).
  2. Show **Peer Cost Benchmark**: Project cost is ₹48 Lakh vs. ₹31.2 Lakh peer median for CC roads.
  3. Show **Evidence Center**: Completion certificate is ❌ Missing (+8 risk points). Click `[VIEW MISSING EVIDENCE]` to open the Deep-Dive Modal.
  4. Show **Similar Works**: 92% match with Work `MPL-2024-UP-004822` located only 420 meters away.

---

### 4. Taking Action & Human-in-the-Loop Governance (1:45 - 2:30)
- **What to say**: *"The AI does not block funds or make accusations. It empowers the officer to act."*
- **Action**:
  1. Click **`[ Request Field Verification ]`**.
  2. Add an investigation note: *"Site inspection assigned to Junior Engineer to verify road length & quality."*
  3. Switch to **Investigations Hub** to show the open case.
  4. Switch to **Audit Logs** to show the immutable cryptographic audit entry.

---

### 5. Report Dossier & Closing (2:30 - 3:00)
- **What to say**: *"With 1 click, we generate an official Statutory Risk Assessment Dossier for CAG or MoSPI audit reviews."*
- **Action**: Open **Reports & Export** and show printable dossier.
- **Closing**: *"From digital tracking to intelligent prioritization—MPLADS Rakshak ensures public funds deliver durable community assets with complete transparency."*
